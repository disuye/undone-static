SMPLR · Music · C++/Qt · FFmpeg

Generative music streaming 24/7

Blurring the lines between producer and consumer; artist and audience: The Infinite Album.

An artistic reaction to big tech ingestion & AI slop output, experimental artist SMPLR pointed a sonic firehose directly at the internet.

Undone worked with the artist turning shell scripts and theoretical ideas into a functional C++/Qt server based application.

SMPLR's self-named application 'SMPLR' creates and streams continuous, non-repeating, non-AI, algorithmically generated experimental ambient music 24/7... forever.

To date, SMPLR's 'The Infinite Album' contains 6000+ hours of unique experimental music, archived to YouTube in ~12 hour chunks.

Based on FFmpeg and other open source technologies, the app deterministically creates music from a sample library, generates an audio-visual broadcast, and handles live streaming via the YouTube API (along with Kick and formerly Twitch), recovery from dropouts and network faults... all without any human intervention.

CFK Media · Web · Three.js · HTML/CSS

Tokyo in the browser

Film and documentary production company CFK came to us with a request for a unique landing page.

Based on location stills from a documentary filmed in Japan, Undone re-created a Tokyo street scene using browser-based procedural 3D technology.

Fast load times, device-dependent resolution, and completely flexible, even able to handle dynamic content.

Includes custom coded grain overlay based on actual photographic film, halation, bloom, aberration and other 'filmic' effects. Even a guest appearance from the mighty Godzilla, if you watch closely.

Undone loves this type of challenge; technology solves a creative issue (websites are cookie cutter), the end result aligns perfectly with CFK's brand, and it makes users smile.

Checkpoint · Audio · C++/Qt · CoreAudio

Real-time audio analysis

Lightweight, fully featured, professional audio analysis tools for macOS.

Undone recently developed Checkpoint, a CPU/GPU efficient, minimalist yet highly customizable suite of audio analysis tools for macOS.

This is not another bloody plug-in! As an in-house tool, Checkpoint allows us to quicky A-B audio files played from different sources. Metric Halo's SpectraFoo was our previous 'go to' suite, but it no longer runs on current macOS. So we built Checkpoint.

Featuring the usual suspects of Meter, Spectrogram and Spectrograph, Phase and Correlation plus Waveform, there is a strong emphasis on mid-side analysis, with a clever "catch-all sound" design which negates the need for manually assigning channel inputs: Play music, run app, win prizes.

Checkpoint is currently in closed beta but will be available here in good time.
